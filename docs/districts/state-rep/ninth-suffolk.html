Massachusetts Ninth Suffolk State Representative District

The Massachusetts Ninth Suffolk State Representative District is currently represented by Democratic incumbent John Francis Moran, who won a special election in 2023 with 100% of the vote. The district is located entirely in Boston, encompassing parts of 18 precincts within the city, and has a diverse population with 56% minority residents and 44% white residents.
