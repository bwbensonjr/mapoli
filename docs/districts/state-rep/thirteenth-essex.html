Massachusetts Thirteenth Essex State Representative District

The Thirteenth Essex State Representative District in Massachusetts covers parts of Danvers, Middleton, Peabody, Topsfield, and Wenham, spanning 30 square miles with a population of 44,833. The district is predominantly white (88%) with a 4% poverty rate and 36% college-educated population. It is currently represented by Democratic incumbent Sally P. Kerans of Danvers, who reclaimed the seat in 2020 after previously serving in the early 1990s.
