Massachusetts Thirty-Fifth Middlesex State Representative District

The Massachusetts Thirty-Fifth Middlesex State Representative District covers parts of Malden and Medford, with a population of 42,781. The district is currently represented by incumbent Paul J. Donato, Sr. (D-Medford), who has won consecutive elections since 2002, often running unopposed.
