Massachusetts Twenty-Fourth Middlesex State Representative District

The Massachusetts Twenty-Fourth Middlesex State Representative District is currently represented by incumbent David M. Rogers (D-Cambridge), who has run unopposed in every election since 2014. The district covers parts of Arlington, Belmont, and Cambridge, encompassing approximately 7 square miles with a population of 46,340.
