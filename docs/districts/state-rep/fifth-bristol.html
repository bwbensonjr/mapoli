Massachusetts Fifth Bristol State Representative District

This Massachusetts Fifth Bristol State Representative District is currently represented by Patricia A. Haddad (D-Somerset), who was recently defeated by Justin Thurber (R-Somerset) in the 2024 election. The district includes parts of Dighton, Somerset, Swansea, and Taunton.
