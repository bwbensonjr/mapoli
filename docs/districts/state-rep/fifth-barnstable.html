Massachusetts Fifth Barnstable State Representative District

The Massachusetts Fifth Barnstable State Representative District covers portions of Barnstable, Bourne, and Sandwich with a population of 41,150. The district is currently represented by Republican incumbent Steven G. Xiarhos of Barnstable, who won reelection in 2024 with 61% of the vote against Democratic challenger Owen G. Fletcher.
