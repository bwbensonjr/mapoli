Massachusetts Second Worcester State Representative District

The Second Worcester State Representative District in Massachusetts is represented by incumbent Democrat Jonathan D. Zlotnik of Gardner, who has held the seat since 2013. The district includes the cities/towns of Ashburnham, Gardner, Templeton, and parts of Winchendon (precincts 2 and 3), covering a mostly rural area in central Massachusetts.
