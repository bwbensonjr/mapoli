Massachusetts Seventh Middlesex State Representative District

The Seventh Middlesex State Representative District is currently represented by Democrat Jack Patrick Lewis of Framingham. The district covers parts of Framingham and Ashland, with a population of about 43,673 people across 23 square miles.
