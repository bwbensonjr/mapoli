Massachusetts Sixteenth Essex State Representative District

This Massachusetts Sixteenth Essex State Representative District is primarily located in the cities of Lawrence and Methuen. The current incumbent is Francisco E. Paulino, a Democrat from Methuen, who was elected in 2022 and is running for reelection in 2024.
