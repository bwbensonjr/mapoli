Massachusetts Tenth Suffolk State Representative District

The Tenth Suffolk State Representative District in Massachusetts encompasses parts of Boston and Brookline, covering about 8 square miles. The current incumbent is William Macgregor (D-Boston), who won a special election in 2023 and was re-elected in 2024. Previously, the district was represented by Edward Coppinger from 2010 to 2022.
