Massachusetts Eighteenth Suffolk State Representative District

The Massachusetts Eighteenth Suffolk State Representative District is currently represented by Democratic incumbent Michael J. Moran of Boston. The district includes portions of Boston and Cambridge, covering approximately 3 square miles with a population of 38,620 residents.
