Massachusetts Sixth Worcester State Representative District

The Massachusetts Sixth Worcester State Representative District encompasses Charlton (precincts 1, 2, 3, 4A), Dudley, Southbridge, and Spencer (precinct 1), covering about 84 square miles with a population of 43,277. The district is currently represented by Republican John J. Marsi, Jr. of Dudley, who won a special election in March 2024 and was reelected in November 2024 with 62% of the vote.
