Massachusetts Fourth Middlesex State Representative District

The Massachusetts Fourth Middlesex State Representative District is currently represented by incumbent Democratic Representative Danielle W. Gregoire of Marlborough. The district encompasses parts of Framingham and Marlborough, with a population of approximately 42,327 people.
