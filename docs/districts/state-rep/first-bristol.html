Massachusetts First Bristol State Representative District

The Massachusetts First Bristol State Representative District is currently represented by Republican Fred Jay Barrows from Mansfield, who has held the seat since winning a 2006 special election. The district includes portions of Foxborough, Mansfield, and Norton in southeastern Massachusetts.

1st Bristol District
Incumbent: Michael Chaisson (R-Foxborough)
