Massachusetts Eleventh Worcester State Representative District

The Massachusetts Eleventh Worcester State Representative District comprises Shrewsbury and parts of Westborough, covering 26 square miles with a population of 42,109. The current incumbent is Republican Hannah E. Kane of Shrewsbury, who has held the seat since winning a special election in 2015.
