Massachusetts Twelfth Bristol State Representative District

The Massachusetts Twelfth Bristol State Representative District covers Berkley, Lakeville, parts of Middleborough (precincts 2, 4, 5, 7), and parts of Taunton (precincts 3-A, 3-B, 4-A, 4-B). The current incumbent is Norman J. Orrall, a Republican from Lakeville, who has held the seat since winning election in 2018.
