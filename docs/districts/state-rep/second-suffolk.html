Massachusetts Second Suffolk State Representative District

The Massachusetts Second Suffolk State Representative District is currently represented by incumbent Daniel J. Ryan (D-Boston), who has held the seat since winning a special election in 2014 and has run unopposed in subsequent elections. The district includes portions of Boston, Cambridge, and Everett, with a population of approximately 42,463 residents.
