Massachusetts Eighth Plymouth State Representative District

The Massachusetts Eighth Plymouth State Representative District is currently represented by Democrat Dennis C. Gallagher of Bridgewater, who won the seat in the 2024 election with 51% of the vote over Republican Sandra Wright. The district encompasses parts of Bridgewater and Raynham, with a population of approximately 44,000 residents.
