Massachusetts Third Plymouth State Representative District

The Massachusetts Third Plymouth State Representative District includes the towns of Cohasset, Hingham, and Hull. The current incumbent is Joan Meschino (D-Hull), who has represented the district since 2016 and has run unopposed in the last three election cycles (2020, 2022, and 2024).
