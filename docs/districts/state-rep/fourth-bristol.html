Massachusetts Fourth Bristol State Representative District

The Massachusetts Fourth Bristol State Representative District covers Norton (precincts 1, 2), Rehoboth, Seekonk, and Swansea (precincts 4, 5), with a predominantly white (91%) population of 41,529. The incumbent is Republican Steven S. Howitt of Seekonk, who has represented the district since 2010 when he defeated Democrat Steven J. D’Amico.
