Massachusetts Eleventh Suffolk State Representative District

The Massachusetts Eleventh Suffolk State Representative District covers parts of Chelsea and Everett, with a diverse population that is predominantly Hispanic (65%). The current incumbent is Democratic Representative Judith A. Garcia of Chelsea, who was first elected in 2022, winning with 73% of the vote against Republican Todd B. Taylor.
