Massachusetts Seventh Plymouth State Representative District

The Massachusetts Seventh Plymouth State Representative District is currently represented by Alyson M. Sullivan-Almeida (Republican) from Abington. The district covers the towns of Abington, Whitman, and parts of East Bridgewater (precincts 1, 2, and 3).
