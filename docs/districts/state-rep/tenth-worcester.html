Massachusetts Tenth Worcester State Representative District

The Massachusetts Tenth Worcester State Representative District includes the towns of Hopedale, Mendon, and Milford. The current incumbent is Brian William Murray, a Democrat from Milford, who has represented the district since 2016 and has won his most recent elections unopposed.
