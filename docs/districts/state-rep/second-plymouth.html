Massachusetts Second Plymouth State Representative District

The Massachusetts Second Plymouth State Representative District is currently represented by Republican John Robert Gaskey from Carver, who won election in 2024 succeeding long-time incumbent Susan Williams Gifford. The district includes the towns of Carver and Wareham, plus parts of Middleborough, with a predominantly white (86%) population of approximately 42,045 residents.
