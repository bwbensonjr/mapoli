Massachusetts Eighth Norfolk State Representative District

The Eighth Norfolk State Representative District in Massachusetts is currently represented by Democrat Ted Philips of Sharon. The district covers portions of Mansfield, Sharon, Stoughton, and Walpole with a population of approximately 43,000 residents.
