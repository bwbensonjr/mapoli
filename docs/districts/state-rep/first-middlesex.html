Massachusetts First Middlesex State Representative District

The Massachusetts First Middlesex State Representative District is currently represented by Democrat Margaret R. Scarsdale of Pepperell, who narrowly won the 2024 election with 51% of the vote. The district covers portions of Middlesex County including the towns of Ashby, Dunstable, parts of Groton, parts of Lunenburg, Pepperell, and Townsend.
