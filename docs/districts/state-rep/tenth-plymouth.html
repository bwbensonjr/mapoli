Massachusetts Tenth Plymouth State Representative District

The Massachusetts Tenth Plymouth State Representative District is located entirely within the city of Brockton, comprising precincts 4-A through 6-D. The district is currently represented by incumbent Democrat Michelle Marie Dubois of Brockton, who has held the seat since 2014.
