Massachusetts Eleventh Middlesex State Representative District

This district is located entirely in Newton, Massachusetts, covering 16 precincts within the city. The current representative is Amy Mah Sangiolo (D-Newton), who won the most recent election in November 2024 with 81% of the vote against Republican challenger Vladislav S. Yanovsky. Prior to Sangiolo, Kay S. Khan (D-Newton) represented the district for many years, often running unopposed.
