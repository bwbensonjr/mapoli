Massachusetts Sixteenth Suffolk State Representative District

The Massachusetts Sixteenth Suffolk State Representative District is represented by incumbent Democrat Jessica Ann Giannino from Revere. The district encompasses most of Revere and a small portion of Saugus, with a population of approximately 40,000 residents.
