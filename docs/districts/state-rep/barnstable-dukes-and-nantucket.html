Massachusetts Barnstable, Dukes and Nantucket State Representative District

The Barnstable, Dukes and Nantucket State Representative District in Massachusetts is currently represented by Democrat Thomas W. Moakley of Falmouth. The district encompasses Martha’s Vineyard, Nantucket, Gosnold, and portions of Falmouth on Cape Cod.
