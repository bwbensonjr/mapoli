Massachusetts Thirteenth Worcester State Representative District

The Thirteenth Worcester State Representative District is located entirely in Worcester, Massachusetts, covering 13 precincts within the city. The district is currently represented by incumbent John J. Mahoney, Jr. (D-Worcester), who has held the seat since 2010 and has run unopposed in most recent elections.
