Massachusetts Eighth Suffolk State Representative District

The Massachusetts Eighth Suffolk State Representative District is currently represented by Democratic incumbent Jay D. Livingstone of Boston, who has held the seat since winning a special election in 2013. The district is located entirely within Boston, covering portions of the city across 18 precincts. It is a compact urban district with an area of just 1 square mile and a population of 41,784.
