Massachusetts Seventh Norfolk State Representative District

The Massachusetts Seventh Norfolk State Representative District covers parts of Milton and Randolph, with a population of 42,319. The district is currently represented by Richard Gerard Wells, Jr. (D-Milton), who won the 2024 election with 65% of the vote against Clinton Graham.
