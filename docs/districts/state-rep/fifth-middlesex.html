Massachusetts Fifth Middlesex State Representative District

The Massachusetts Fifth Middlesex State Representative District is currently represented by incumbent David Paul Linsky (D-Natick), who has held the seat since 1999. The district encompasses parts of Natick and precincts 2 and 3 of Wayland.
