Massachusetts Sixth Norfolk State Representative District

The Massachusetts Sixth Norfolk State Representative District encompasses Avon, Canton, and parts of Stoughton, with a population of 43,712. The district is currently represented by William C. Galvin (D-Canton), who has consistently won elections since at least 1990, often running unopposed in recent elections.
