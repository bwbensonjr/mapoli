Massachusetts Fifteenth Middlesex State Representative District

The Fifteenth Middlesex State Representative District in Massachusetts includes portions of Lexington, Winchester, and Woburn, with a population of 41,829. The district is currently represented by incumbent Democrat Michelle Ciccolo of Lexington, who has held the seat since 2018 and has run unopposed in recent elections.
