Massachusetts Fifth Essex State Representative District

The Massachusetts Fifth Essex State Representative District encompasses Essex, Gloucester, Manchester-by-the-Sea, and Rockport, with a population of approximately 44,000. It is currently represented by incumbent Ann-Margaret Ferrante, a Democrat from Gloucester who has held the seat since 2008 and most recently won reelection in 2024 with 67% of the vote against an unenrolled candidate.
