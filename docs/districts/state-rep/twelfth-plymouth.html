Massachusetts Twelfth Plymouth State Representative District

The Twelfth Plymouth State Representative District in Massachusetts includes parts of Halifax, Kingston, Middleborough, Pembroke, Plymouth, and Plympton, covering 77 square miles with a population of 45,094. The district is currently represented by incumbent Democrat Kathleen R. LaNatra of Kingston, who was most recently reelected in 2024.
