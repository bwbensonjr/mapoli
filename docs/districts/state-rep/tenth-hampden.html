Massachusetts Tenth Hampden State Representative District

The Massachusetts Tenth Hampden State Representative District is located entirely in Springfield and is currently represented by Democratic incumbent Carlos Gonzalez of Springfield. The district has a predominantly minority population (84%) with high poverty rates (42%), and Gonzalez has been re-elected without opposition since first winning the seat in 2014.
