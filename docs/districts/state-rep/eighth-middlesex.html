Massachusetts Eighth Middlesex State Representative District

The Massachusetts Eighth Middlesex State Representative District includes Holliston, Hopkinton, parts of Millis (precincts 2, 3), and Sherborn. The incumbent is Democrat James Arena-DeRosa of Holliston, who was first elected in 2022 and is running unopposed for reelection in 2024.
