Massachusetts Fifth Hampden State Representative District

The Fifth Hampden State Representative District in Massachusetts encompasses parts of Chicopee and Holyoke, covering 23 square miles with a population of 42,111. The current incumbent is Patricia A. Duffy, a Democrat from Holyoke, who has represented the district since 2020 after winning uncontested elections.
