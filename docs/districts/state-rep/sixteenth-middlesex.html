Massachusetts Sixteenth Middlesex State Representative District

The Massachusetts Sixteenth Middlesex State Representative District covers parts of Chelmsford and Lowell, with a population of 43,352. The current incumbent is Democratic Representative Rodney M. Elliott of Lowell, who was elected in 2022 with 65% of the vote and is running unopposed in the 2024 election.
