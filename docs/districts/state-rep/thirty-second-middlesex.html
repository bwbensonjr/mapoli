Massachusetts Thirty-Second Middlesex State Representative District

The Massachusetts Thirty-Second Middlesex State Representative District covers parts of Malden, all of Melrose, and precincts 4, 5, and 6 in Wakefield. The district is currently represented by Democratic incumbent Kate Lipper-Garabedian of Melrose, who has held the seat since winning a special election in March 2020.
