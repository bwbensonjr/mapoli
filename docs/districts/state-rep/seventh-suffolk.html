Massachusetts Seventh Suffolk State Representative District

The Massachusetts Seventh Suffolk State Representative District covers several precincts in Boston, encompassing approximately 2 square miles with a population of 39,463. The district is currently represented by incumbent Chynah Tyler (D-Boston), who has won every election since 2016, with her predecessor Gloria L. Fox (D-Boston) having represented the district from at least 1990 to 2014.
