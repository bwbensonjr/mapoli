Massachusetts Fifth Norfolk State Representative District

The Fifth Norfolk State Representative District is located in Massachusetts and includes parts of Braintree and precincts 1 and 2 of Holbrook. The current incumbent is Mark J. Cusack, a Democrat from Braintree who has held this seat since 2010 and ran unopposed in the 2022 and 2024 elections.
