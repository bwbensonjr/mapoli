Massachusetts Fourth Plymouth State Representative District

The Fourth Plymouth State Representative District in Massachusetts is represented by incumbent Patrick Joseph Kearney (D-Scituate). The district covers parts of Marshfield, Norwell, and Scituate on Massachusetts’ South Shore region.
