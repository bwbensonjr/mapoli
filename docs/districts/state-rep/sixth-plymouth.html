Massachusetts Sixth Plymouth State Representative District

The Massachusetts Sixth Plymouth State Representative District covers Duxbury, parts of Halifax, Hanson, Marshfield, and Pembroke, encompassing an area of 66 square miles with a population of 44,620. Republican Kenneth Sweezey is the current representative, having defeated Democrat Rebecca Coletta in the 2024 election with 53% of the vote.
