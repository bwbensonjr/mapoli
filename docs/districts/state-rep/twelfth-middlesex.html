Massachusetts Twelfth Middlesex State Representative District

The Massachusetts Twelfth Middlesex State Representative District includes parts of Brookline and Newton, with a population of 44,561. The current incumbent is Democrat Greg Schwartz from Newton, who will represent the district following the 2024 election after a long tenure by Ruth B. Balser, who had represented the district since at least 2002.
