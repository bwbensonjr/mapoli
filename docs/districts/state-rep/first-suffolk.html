Massachusetts First Suffolk State Representative District

The First Suffolk State Representative District is a legislative district in Massachusetts covering parts of Boston, represented by incumbent Democrat Adrian C. Madaro. The district has a diverse population that is predominantly Hispanic (55%), with a total minority population of 66%.
