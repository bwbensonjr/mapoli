Massachusetts Fifteenth Essex State Representative District

The Massachusetts Fifteenth Essex State Representative District is represented by Ryan M. Hamilton (D-Methuen), who has won uncontested races in 2022 and 2024. The district covers parts of Haverhill and Methuen, with a population of 46,016 that is 70% white and 30% minority.
