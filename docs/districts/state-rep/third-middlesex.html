Massachusetts Third Middlesex State Representative District

The Massachusetts Third Middlesex State Representative District is currently represented by incumbent Democrat Kate Hogan of Stow, who has held the seat since 2008. The district encompasses the towns of Bolton, Hudson, Maynard, and Stow in Middlesex County.
