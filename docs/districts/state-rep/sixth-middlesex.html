Massachusetts Sixth Middlesex State Representative District

The Massachusetts Sixth Middlesex State Representative District encompasses Framingham with a diverse population of 40,033 residents. The current incumbent is Democrat Priscila S. Sousa from Framingham, who won reelection in 2022 and 2024 with 100% of the vote.
