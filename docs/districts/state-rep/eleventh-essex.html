Massachusetts Eleventh Essex State Representative District

The Massachusetts Eleventh Essex State Representative District is located in Lynn and Nahant, with a majority-minority population (63%). The incumbent is Democrat Sean Reid of Lynn, who succeeded Peter L. Capano in 2024.
