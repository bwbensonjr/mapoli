Massachusetts Eighth Essex State Representative District

The Eighth Essex State Representative District is a Massachusetts legislative district covering parts of Lynn, Marblehead, and Swampscott. The incumbent is Jennifer Wb Armini (Democrat from Marblehead), who has held the position since 2022.
