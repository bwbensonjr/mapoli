Massachusetts Second Hampden State Representative District

The Massachusetts Second Hampden State Representative District is represented by incumbent Brian M. Ashe (D-Longmeadow), who has held the seat since 2008. The district includes parts of East Longmeadow, Hampden, Longmeadow, Monson, and Springfield, covering approximately 72 square miles in western Massachusetts.
