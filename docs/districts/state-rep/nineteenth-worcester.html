Massachusetts Nineteenth Worcester State Representative District

The Nineteenth Worcester State Representative District is currently represented by Kate Donaghue (D-Westborough), who won reelection in 2024 with 78% of the vote. The district includes portions of Framingham, Northborough, Southborough, and Westborough, covering 47 square miles with a population of 41,148.
