Massachusetts Seventh Worcester State Representative District

This district covers parts of Auburn, Charlton, Leicester, Millbury, and Oxford. It has a population of 43,511 and is predominantly white (89%). The current incumbent is Paul K. Frost, a Republican from Auburn, who has held this seat since 1997 and has been consistently re-elected, most recently in 2022 with 75% of the vote against Terry Burke Dotson.
