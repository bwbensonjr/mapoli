Massachusetts Sixth Essex State Representative District

The district encompasses Beverly and Wenham, with a population of 43,831. The incumbent is Democratic State Representative Jerald A. Parisella of Beverly, who has represented the district since 2010, consistently winning reelection, most recently with 71% of the vote in 2024.
