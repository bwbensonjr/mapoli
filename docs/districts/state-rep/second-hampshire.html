Massachusetts Second Hampshire State Representative District

The Massachusetts Second Hampshire State Representative District includes Easthampton, parts of Granby, Hadley, and South Hadley, with a population of 41,674. The current incumbent is Democrat Homar Gomez of Easthampton, who will represent the district following the 2024 election.
