Massachusetts Ninth Plymouth State Representative District

The Massachusetts Ninth Plymouth State Representative District encompasses parts of Brockton, East Bridgewater, Easton, and West Bridgewater, with a population of 46,626. The current representative is Democrat Bridget M. Plouffe of Brockton, who was elected in 2024, succeeding longtime Democratic representative Gerard J. Cassidy.
