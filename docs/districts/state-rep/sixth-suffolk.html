Massachusetts Sixth Suffolk State Representative District

The Massachusetts Sixth Suffolk State Representative District is located in Boston and is currently represented by Democratic incumbent Russell E. Holmes. The district is predominantly minority (91%) with a significant Black population (57%) and has been consistently held by Democratic representatives who have run unopposed in elections since 2010.
