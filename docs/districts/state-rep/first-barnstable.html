Massachusetts First Barnstable State Representative District

The Massachusetts First Barnstable State Representative District encompasses the towns of Brewster and Dennis, along with precincts 1, 2, 3, 4, 7, and 8 of Yarmouth on Cape Cod. The current incumbent is Democrat Christopher Richard Flanagan of Dennis, who was first elected in 2022 and won re-election in 2024 with 57% of the vote.
