Massachusetts Thirty-Sixth Middlesex State Representative District

The Thirty-Sixth Middlesex State Representative District in Massachusetts includes the towns of Dracut and Tyngsborough. It is currently represented by Democrat Colleen M. Garry of Dracut, who has held this seat since at least 2002 and has won several elections, sometimes unopposed.

36th Middlesex District
Incumbent: Colleen M. Garry (D-Dracut)
