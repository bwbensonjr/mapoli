Massachusetts Sixteenth Worcester State Representative District

Massachusetts Sixteenth Worcester State Representative District encompasses 13 precincts in Worcester. The current incumbent is Democrat Daniel M. Donahue of Worcester, who has represented the district since winning a special election in 2013 and has run unopposed in most recent elections.
