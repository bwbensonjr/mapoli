Massachusetts Eighth U.S. House District

This district, which includes parts of Boston and several towns south of the city, has been represented by incumbent Democratic Representative Stephen F. Lynch of Boston since 2013. It covers portions of Boston and surrounding communities including Quincy, Brockton, and other municipalities in eastern Massachusetts.
