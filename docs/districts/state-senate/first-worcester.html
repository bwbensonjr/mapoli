Massachusetts First Worcester State Senate District

The First Worcester State Senate District is currently represented by Robyn K. Kennedy (D-Worcester), who is running unopposed in the 2024 election. The district encompasses parts of Worcester and several surrounding towns, with 58% white and 42% minority population, covering an area of 107 square miles.
