Massachusetts Norfolk and Suffolk State Senate District

The Norfolk and Suffolk State Senate District in Massachusetts is represented by incumbent Michael F. Rush (D-Boston), who has run unopposed since at least 2012. The district encompasses parts of Boston as well as Dedham, Norwood, Walpole, and Westwood, with a population of 183,656 that is predominantly white (69%) with 31% minority residents.
