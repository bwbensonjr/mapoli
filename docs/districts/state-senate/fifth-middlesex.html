Massachusetts Fifth Middlesex State Senate District

The Massachusetts Fifth Middlesex State Senate District is currently represented by Democrat Jason M. Lewis of Winchester, who has held the seat since winning a special election in 2014. The district includes Malden, Melrose, Reading, Stoneham, Wakefield, and part of Winchester, covering 37 square miles in the northeastern suburbs of Boston.
