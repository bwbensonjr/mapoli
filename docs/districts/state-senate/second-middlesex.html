Massachusetts Second Middlesex State Senate District

The Massachusetts Second Middlesex State Senate District is currently represented by Democratic incumbent Patricia D. Jehlen of Somerville, who has held the seat since winning a special election in 2005. The district covers parts of Cambridge, Medford, Somerville, and Winchester, with a population of approximately 180,000 residents.
