Massachusetts Second Suffolk State Senate District

This district is currently represented by incumbent State Senator Liz Miranda (D-Boston). The district encompasses parts of Boston, including sections of Dorchester, Roxbury, and parts of Jamaica Plain, with a diverse population that is 80% minority and 20% white.
