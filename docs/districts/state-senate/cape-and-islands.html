Massachusetts Cape and Islands State Senate District

The Massachusetts Cape and Islands State Senate District covers the Cape Cod region, Martha’s Vineyard, and Nantucket, spanning 19 towns with a total population of 169,739 across 407 square miles. The current incumbent is Julian Andre Cyr, a Democrat from Provincetown who has represented the district since 2017 and was most recently reelected in 2024.
