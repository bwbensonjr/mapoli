Massachusetts Second Plymouth and Norfolk State Senate District

The Massachusetts Second Plymouth and Norfolk State Senate District covers Avon, Brockton, East Bridgewater, Halifax, Hanson, parts of Randolph (precincts 1, 2, 3, 7, 8), and Whitman. The current incumbent is Michael D. Brady (D-Brockton), who won his most recent election in 2022 with 64% of the vote against Republican challenger Jim Gordon.
