Massachusetts Norfolk, Worcester and Middlesex State Senate District

The Norfolk, Worcester and Middlesex State Senate District is currently represented by Democratic incumbent Rebecca L. Rausch of Needham, who won re-election in 2024 with 59% of the vote. The district spans 11 communities across three counties including Bellingham, Dover, Franklin, Medfield, Milford, Millis, Needham, Norfolk, Plainville, Sherborn, and Wrentham.
