Massachusetts Worcester and Hampden State Senate District

This is a Massachusetts state senate district covering multiple towns in central Massachusetts, including Blackstone, Charlton, Dudley, and several others. The current incumbent is Ryan C. Fattman, a Republican from Sutton who won reelection in 2024 with 66% of the vote against Democratic challenger Anthony Allard.
