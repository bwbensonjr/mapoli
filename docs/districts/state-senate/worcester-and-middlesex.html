Massachusetts Worcester and Middlesex State Senate District

The Massachusetts Worcester and Middlesex State Senate District is currently represented by incumbent John J. Cronin (D-Fitchburg), who was reelected in 2024 with 59% of the vote. The district encompasses ten municipalities in north-central Massachusetts including Fitchburg, Leominster, Clinton, and portions of Middlesex County.
