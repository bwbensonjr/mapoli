Massachusetts Hampden State Senate District

The Massachusetts Hampden State Senate District is currently represented by Adam Gomez (D-Springfield), who has been in office since 2020 and was most recently reelected unopposed in 2022. The district covers parts of Springfield and Chicopee, with a diverse population that is 69% minority (48% Hispanic, 16% Black) and has a 25% poverty rate.
