Massachusetts Suffolk and Middlesex State Senate District

This Massachusetts state senate district, encompassing parts of Boston, Cambridge, Belmont, and Watertown, is currently represented by incumbent Democrat William N. Brownsberger from Belmont. The district has a population of approximately 174,633 residents and covers 17 square miles in the eastern part of the state.
