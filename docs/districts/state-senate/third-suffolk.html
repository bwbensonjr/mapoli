Massachusetts Third Suffolk State Senate District

This Massachusetts state senate district includes parts of Boston, with a population of approximately 176,801 people. The incumbent is Lydia Marie Edwards (D-Boston), who won reelection in 2024 with 70% of the vote against Republican challenger Jeannamarie Tamas.
