Massachusetts Middlesex and Suffolk State Senate District

The Massachusetts Middlesex and Suffolk State Senate District is represented by incumbent Sal N. DiDomenico (D-Everett), who has won his elections with 100% of the vote since at least 2012. The district covers parts of Boston and Cambridge, as well as Chelsea and Everett, with a diverse population that is 56% minority residents.
