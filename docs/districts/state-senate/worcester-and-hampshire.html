Massachusetts Worcester and Hampshire State Senate District

This state senate district, currently represented by Peter J. Durant (R-Spencer) who won the seat in a 2023 special election, covers approximately 617 square miles of central Massachusetts. The district includes 21 municipalities, primarily rural and suburban towns in Worcester and Hampshire counties, along with several precincts in the city of Worcester.
