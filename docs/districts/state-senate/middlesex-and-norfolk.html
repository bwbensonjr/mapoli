Massachusetts Middlesex and Norfolk State Senate District

The Massachusetts Middlesex and Norfolk State Senate District, represented by incumbent Karen E. Spilka (D-Ashland), encompasses the cities/towns of Ashland, Framingham, Holliston, Hopkinton, Medway, and Natick. Spilka has won re-election unopposed in the most recent elections.
