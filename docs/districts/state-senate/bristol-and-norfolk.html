Massachusetts Bristol and Norfolk State Senate District

The Massachusetts Bristol and Norfolk State Senate District is currently represented by Democratic incumbent Paul R. Feeney from Foxborough. The district covers parts of southeastern Massachusetts including Attleboro, Canton, Foxborough, Mansfield, North Attleborough, Norton, and Sharon.
