Massachusetts Second Bristol and Plymouth State Senate District

The Massachusetts Second Bristol and Plymouth State Senate District is represented by long-serving Democratic incumbent Mark C. Montigny of New Bedford. The district encompasses parts of southeastern Massachusetts including Acushnet, Dartmouth, Fairhaven, Mattapoisett, and New Bedford.
