Massachusetts Sixth Governor’s Council District

The Massachusetts Sixth Governor’s Council District covers several municipalities including Boston, Cambridge, Chelsea, Everett, and other communities in eastern Massachusetts. The district has a population of 899,584. The current incumbent is Terrence W. Kennedy (D-Lynnfield), who has held the seat since 2010 and has been re-elected multiple times, often running unopposed.
