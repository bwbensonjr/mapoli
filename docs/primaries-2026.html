September 2026 Primary Candidates

Democratic and Republican candidates in contested Massachusetts legislative primaries for the September 1, 2026 state primary, by district.

Candidates on the ballot for the September 1, 2026 Massachusetts state primary, for every legislative district with a contested primary — that is, where more than one candidate of the same party is running. Districts where both parties field a single candidate are not listed.

Where a district is contested for one party only, the other party’s candidate is shown marked unopposed. Sitting legislators are marked incumbent, and seats whose incumbent is not seeking re-election are flagged as open. District names link to the full district page with maps, demographics, and election history.
