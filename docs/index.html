Fun Facts

The largest and smallest State Rep districts

  • While every mapoli State Representative district has approximately the same population (currently about 44,000 people) the districts vary widely in geographic size.
  • The smallest State Representative district in terms of area is Ways and Means Chair Aaron Michlewitz’s Third Suffolk district coming in at a petite 1.36 square miles.
  • The largest State Representative district is the Third Berkshire district represented by Leigh Davis of Great Barrington. The district covers a whopping 551 square miles and contains 18 separate municipalities.
